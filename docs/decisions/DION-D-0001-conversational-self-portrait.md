# DION-D-0001: Conversational self-portrait laboratory

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-07-23

## Context

A distinct unmet need exists: carefully designed, voice-friendly interviews
that help a person build a revisable portrait with explicit evidence,
disagreement, and human review.

## Decision

Establish Dionysus around conversational interview protocols and
evidence-grounded personal portrait formats.

Keep the active public root limited to protocol, contracts, fictional examples,
and validation. Real personal material belongs to an ignored private vault.
Voice is the preferred interaction mode, but the contracts remain transport and
provider neutral.

Treat claims as proposed interpretations until a person reviews them. Treat
portrait documents as disposable, purpose-bounded projections rather than one
canonical biography.

## Consequences

- The repository can be active without storing a person's biography publicly.
- Interview design can evolve before committing to a voice or storage stack.
- Generic memory, session archives, and runtime integration remain with their
  stronger owners.
- Any later real-data pilot requires a separate privacy and threat-model
  decision.
