# DION-D-0001: Recharter Dionysus as a conversational self-portrait laboratory

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-07-23

## Context

Dionysus previously mediated seed expansion and staging for AoA and Tree of
Sophia. Direct planting by the operator and stronger owner-local workflows made
that intermediary role obsolete. Keeping the old root active would preserve
cost and ambiguity without preserving unique authority.

At the same time, a distinct unmet need exists: carefully designed,
voice-friendly interviews that help a person build a revisable portrait with
explicit evidence, disagreement, and human review.

## Decision

Recharter the active repository around conversational interview protocols and
evidence-grounded personal portrait formats.

Move the complete former seed garden, without content edits, under
`legacy/seed-garden/`. Preserve its Git history and the
`pre-archive-2026-07-23` tag.

Keep the active public root limited to protocol, contracts, fictional examples,
and validation. Real personal material belongs to an ignored private vault.
Voice is the preferred interaction mode, but the contracts remain transport and
provider neutral.

Treat claims as proposed interpretations until a person reviews them. Treat
portrait documents as disposable, purpose-bounded projections rather than one
canonical biography.

## Consequences

- Seed-garden automation is no longer an active Dionysus responsibility.
- The repository can be active without storing a person's biography publicly.
- Interview design can evolve before committing to a voice or storage stack.
- Generic memory, session archives, and runtime integration remain with their
  stronger owners.
- Any later real-data pilot requires a separate privacy and threat-model
  decision.
