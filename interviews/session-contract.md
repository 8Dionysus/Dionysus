# Interview session contract

## Before inquiry

The interviewer must make the following explicit:

1. the purpose and selected protocol;
2. whether audio, transcript, evidence slices, claims, or projections are
   retained;
3. who or what may read retained artifacts;
4. how the person can pause, skip, correct, contest, or end the session;
5. what happens at the close.

Refusal or partial consent must not prevent access to unrelated capabilities.

## Optional instrument orientation

If an instrument result is used to orient the session, the interviewer must
name its registry ID, exact form, language, administration mode, intended use,
and relevant interpretation limits. The person may decline to discuss it.

The interviewer must present the result as a hypothesis source, not as a fact
or diagnosis. Ask where the suggested pattern holds, where it fails, and which
episodes support either reading. Do not administer `external-only`, `pilot`, or
`excluded` content as though it were an admitted Dionysus form.

When the private result is retained, the session manifest records only its
registry ID, exact form, language, mode, disposition at use, purpose, and
private locator. It never embeds responses or scores.

## Conversational loop

### Open

Begin with a broad invitation appropriate to the selected lens. Do not seed a
diagnosis or desired self-description in the question.

### Ground

Ask for a concrete episode, choice, relationship, or artifact. Clarify time,
conditions, other actors, and what the person actually did or noticed.

### Deepen

Explore meaning, causes, consequences, uncertainty, and how the person's view
changed. Prefer the person's language over an imported typology.

### Counter

Seek an exception, alternate explanation, or context in which the apparent
pattern does not hold. The goal is calibrated scope, not adversarial
cross-examination.

### Reflect

Offer a small number of candidate claims in plain language. Distinguish direct
self-statement from interviewer inference and name uncertainty.

### Review

For every candidate claim, invite one of:

- accept as written;
- edit;
- contest while retaining both readings;
- defer;
- reject and do not retain.

Silence is not acceptance.

### Close

Summarize retained artifacts, unresolved tensions, and any agreed follow-up.
Renew the retention choice before saving material.

## Interviewer constraints

The interviewer must not:

- infer character, health, credibility, or emotion from vocal prosody;
- turn a single episode into a global trait without review;
- optimize for a coherent or flattering portrait;
- pressure disclosure after a refusal;
- conceal uncertainty or alternate explanations;
- silently import claims from unrelated personal data;
- promise clinical, legal, or diagnostic validity.

## Evidence anchoring

Evidence references must point to a stable private artifact and a bounded
location, such as a transcript turn range. A claim may cite multiple evidence
slices and counterevidence. Public fixtures may use fictional `vault://`
locators; real locators stay private.

## Session completion

A session is complete only when its capture state, consent state, artifact
references, and review state are recorded. A transcript alone is not a
completed portrait update.
